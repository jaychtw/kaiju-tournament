# Source generation record

All drawings and the arena plate were produced with the built-in image-generation/editing capability using the approved project artwork. No outside art sites, stock/game characters, video-generation service, paid purchase, or commissioned artist was used. Model identifier and seed are not exposed. Prompts are instructions, not a guarantee of consistency or exact regeneration.

Raw outputs are preserved. The frame build crops separate foreground components and performs the disclosed alpha-only cleanup; it does not paint anatomy, produce in-betweens, or deform/rotate a whole image. A source drawing's availability does not imply artistic acceptance.

The exported animation is reproducible from the saved PNGs, source registration, and timeline. Regenerating identical PNGs from prompts is not reproducible and is not claimed.
